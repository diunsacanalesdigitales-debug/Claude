# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Retail shoppers in Honduras, from casual World Cup fans to committed sticker-album collectors, browsing on phones as much as desktop, buying through Diunsa (a mainstream department-store chain, not a niche hobby shop). They land here already wanting to complete their FIFA World Cup 2026 album and need to feel confident reserving a physical product they'll pay for and pick up later, sight unseen at reservation time.

## Product Purpose

Drive preventa (pre-order) reservations for Panini's FIFA World Cup 2026 Official Sticker Collection Update Set, sold exclusively through Diunsa stores in Honduras. The page is the first step of a larger flow: reservation data submitted here writes to a database and triggers downstream automation (a PHP endpoint calling scripts run through Postman) that generates payment and fulfillment steps. Success is a completed, validated reservation: name, 13-digit national ID, pickup store, email, and 8-digit phone.

## Brand Personality

Urgent, energetic, fan-first. This is a limited sticker drop tied to a global tournament, sold at mainstream retail. The tone should feel like the excitement of a World Cup release, not a corporate order form: confident, fast-moving, unapologetically sales-forward, while staying legible and trustworthy enough that shoppers feel safe handing over an ID number and phone.

## Anti-references

No specific sites to avoid beyond the general AI-template look: generic SaaS-style hero-metric layouts, uniform three-card feature grids, purple gradients, or anything that reads as a corporate form rather than a retail sticker-drop campaign.

## Design Principles

- Sales-forward first: every section should move a shopper closer to reserving, not just inform them.
- Earn trust deliberately on the preventa terms: be explicit that reserving secures inventory, not delivery priority, so shoppers aren't misled and support isn't stuck explaining it later.
- Keep the brand system locked: Diunsa blue as the structural/trust color, Panini yellow as the one sales accent, white/grey as the clean base. No new colors introduced.
- Respect the retail reality: shoppers are on mixed connection quality and a range of devices, so motion and imagery should feel energetic without ever blocking or slowing the reservation flow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA. No additional constraints specified.
