# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Honduran retail shoppers (Diunsa customers) browsing primarily on mobile, registering for early access to an upcoming product launch (appliances, electronics). They're price- and promotion-aware, drawn in by the promise of an exclusive coupon and priority access.

## Product Purpose

A single-page early-access registration/waitlist for a Diunsa launch: build anticipation via a countdown, capture contact + preference data via a multi-section form, and convert visitors into registered leads.

## Brand Personality

Premium, modern, high-tech — evokes a Samsung/Apple product-launch feel (confidence, anticipation, polish) — while staying recognizably Diunsa: warm, approachable, rooted in the brand's red/navy/amber palette rather than cold or corporate.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI/SaaS-template look: boxy identical cards, cream/parchment backgrounds, gradient text, small uppercase "eyebrow" labels above every section, numbered 01/02/03 scaffolding.
- Overly corporate or clinical — should not feel cold or impersonal.
- Gimmicky/flashy motion or neon — premium restraint over novelty.

## Design Principles

- Product-launch confidence: generous whitespace, a refined type scale, and restraint over decoration.
- Brand-rooted premium: extend Diunsa's red/navy/amber palette with depth (gradients, subtle glass, shadow) rather than replacing it with a generic tech palette.
- Mobile-first clarity: the registration form is the conversion point — it must stay scannable and fast to complete on a phone.
- Purposeful motion: micro-interactions confirm actions (focus, check, submit) without becoming decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, visible focus states, form errors announced and associated with fields, reduced-motion alternative for all animation.
